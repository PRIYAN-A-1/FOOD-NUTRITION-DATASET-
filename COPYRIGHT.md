## 3. Data Provenance & Third-Party Material

The underlying food nutrition measurements and portion weights originate from public domain datasets published by the **U.S. Department of Agriculture (USDA), Agricultural Research Service, FoodData Central**:

- **USDA SR Legacy** (April 2018)
- **USDA FNDDS 2021–2023** (October 31, 2024 archive)
- **USDA Foundation Foods** (April 30, 2026 archive)

All original USDA record IDs, publication dates, and source citations are explicitly retained. See [`SOURCES.md`](SOURCES.md) for full provenance metadata and SHA-256 checksums.
